How to use the shoe size converter

  1. Open the first list and choose the system your starting number comes from: EU, UK, US men's, US women's, or a foot length in centimeters.

  4. Change the system in the list and the result updates straight away, which makes it easy to compare an EU size from a European website with the UK and US sizes you normally wear.
  5. Use Copy result to paste the sizes into a message or order note, or Clear to start again.

Why shoe sizes differ between countries

Shoe sizing grew up separately in different places. The UK system dates back to the 14th century and counts in barleycorns, each one third of an inch, which is why a UK size step is one third of an inch. The US system is derived from the British one but starts from a different zero point, and it uses separate scales for men and women. The European system, often called Paris points, measures in units of two thirds of a centimeter, and a size is roughly the length of the shoe last in those units, including some room for the toes.

Because of these different starting points, a man who wears a UK 8 usually wears a US men's 9 and an EU 42 to 43, while a woman wearing a US women's 10 would wear about a UK 8, an EU 41 to 42 and a US men's 8.5. This tool applies fixed offsets between the systems, which matches the most common published charts for adult sizes.

Men's versus women's sizes in the US

US women's sizes run roughly 1.5 sizes higher than US men's for the same foot, so a men's 8 is close to a women's 9.5. When a pair is listed as "unisex", brands normally use the men's scale, so women often need to go up one and a half sizes from their usual size to buy it. Both are shown in the results so you can shop across sections without guessing. For children, sizing uses separate kids' scales that this adult-focused tool does not cover.

Measuring your foot for the best fit

The most reliable way to find your size is to measure your feet, and then use the foot-length option in this tool. Do it in the evening, when feet are slightly larger. Stand on a sheet of paper against a wall with your heel touching the wall, mark the tip of your longest toe, and measure the distance from the wall to the mark in centimeters. Repeat with the other foot, because most people have one foot a little longer, and use the larger value. Enter that number with Foot length (cm) selected. The tool adds a comfort allowance of 1.5 centimeters for the toes and rounds to the nearest half size.

  • Wear the socks you usually wear with the shoes you are buying.
  • If the number falls between two sizes, choose the larger one for closed shoes and boots.
  • Wide feet may need a wider fit rather than a longer size; length is only part of the picture.
  • Athletic and running shoes often fit snugger or looser than dress shoes, so many people size up half a size for running.

Why the results are approximate

There is no official worldwide standard for converting shoe sizes. An international system called Mondopoint (ISO 9407) measures foot length in millimeters, but few brands use it as their label. Each manufacturer builds its shoes on its own lasts, so a size 9 in one brand can be noticeably tighter than a size 9 in another, and a half size either way is normal. The converter gives you a reliable starting point, but you should compare your result with the specific size chart of the brand, especially for expensive purchases, online orders and anything you cannot easily return.

Online shopping tips

When ordering from a foreign store, check which system the size chart uses and whether it lists men's or women's. Read reviews that mention "runs small" or "true to size". Look at the stated interior length in centimeters if the page gives one, and compare it with your measurement. Keep in mind that returns may be costly across borders, so a careful conversion is worth the minute it takes. The tool is also handy when you buy gifts, when you travel, or when you shop for sports shoes that are sized differently from your everyday pair.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert US shoe size to EU?

For adults, a US men's size plus 33 gives the EU size, so men's 9 is about EU 42. For women, add 31.5, so a women's 8 is about EU 39.5. Choose the right option in the first list and the tool shows all conversions at once.

What is the difference between US men's and women's sizes?

The women's scale runs about 1.5 sizes higher for the same foot length. A men's 8 corresponds to a women's 9.5. Both appear in the results.

How do I convert UK shoe size to US?

A UK size is generally 1 size lower than the US men's size and about 2.5 sizes lower than the US women's size. For example UK 8 is roughly US men's 9 and US women's 10.5.

Are the conversions exact?

No. There is no single official standard, so values are approximate and brands vary by up to half a size. Use the result as a starting point and check the retailer's own size chart before buying.

How do I find my size from my foot length?

Measure from your heel to the tip of your longest toe in centimeters, choose Foot length (cm) in the tool and enter it. The converter adds 1.5 cm of room for the toes and rounds to the nearest half size. Measure both feet and use the longer one.

What is a Paris point?

It is the unit used in European sizing, equal to two thirds of a centimeter. Multiplying the shoe length in centimeters (foot length plus allowance) by 1.5 gives the European size.

Should I size up if I am between two sizes?

For closed shoes, boots and sneakers it is usually more comfortable to choose the larger size, because feet swell during the day. For sandals or backless shoes a slightly smaller size may feel fine.

Does the tool cover children's sizes?

It is designed for adult sizes. Children's shoes use separate scales that start again at small numbers and change quickly as feet grow, so use the brand's kids' chart or measure the foot length instead.
